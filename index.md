# First Chair Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 10 November 2026
**App:** First Chair (Android, package `app.firstchair.first_chair`)
**Developer:** Andrew Brown
**Contact:** aabrown@gmail.com

First Chair is a private ski and snowboard tracker. It is built so that your
data stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and
no analytics.

## Data the app handles

All of the following is stored only on your device, in the app's private
storage:

- **Location.** While you record a session, the app reads your precise
  location (GPS) and, if your phone has one, its barometer and motion
  sensors. It uses them to work out your track, speed, vertical, runs and
  lifts. Recording can continue with the screen off; Android shows a
  notification while it does.
- **Session details you enter.** Notes, ratings, companions, snow and weather
  details, run edits and similar.
- **Photos you choose to add.** The app reads the photos you pick, including
  the time they were taken, so it can place them on the map. It does not
  copy them off your device.
- **Files you import.** SkiTracks, GPX, KML and First Chair backup files you
  select.
- **Settings.** Your preferences, such as units and theme.

## What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any server we run. We do not
have servers that receive your data, and we cannot see it.

The only network activity is the map. To draw maps the app downloads map
images ("tiles") from these public services:

- OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org)
- OpenTopoMap (opentopomap.org)
- OpenSnowMap (opensnowmap.org)

These services receive your device's IP address and the map tiles being
requested, which indicate the general area you are viewing. We do not send
them your tracks, your identity or any other data. Their own privacy policies
apply to what they do with those requests.

## Sharing you start yourself

If you use the share or export buttons, the app hands the file you chose
(for example a session or a backup) to the app you pick, such as email or a
cloud drive. That is your choice, and the receiving app's policy applies.
The photo slideshow Cast button shows photos on a TV using Android's own
casting.

## Permissions

- **Location:** to record your sessions.
- **Foreground service (location) and notifications:** to keep recording
  with the screen off and show that it is running.
- **Battery optimization exemption (optional):** so Android does not pause
  recording while you ski.
- **Internet:** to load map tiles.
- **Photos:** only the ones you select.

The app does not request background location access. Location is only read
while a recording is running.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays on the device until you delete it. You can delete a session
inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data. Backup
files you create are yours to keep or delete.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
data from anyone, as nothing is collected by us.
